Privacy Policy

Tessera · Strathmore News Plus Ltd · Effective 6 October 2026

РусскийEnglish

Tessera sends almost nothing. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising networks. Everything you create in the field journal stays in the app's storage on your device. Its one network request checks the time of a new journal page with our server; the device advertising identifier goes with that request only if you allow tracking.

Information that is sent

When a new page is filed in the field journal, the game asks our server for the current time (api.platform-core.com) so the page can show a checked time. Before the first such request, iOS asks whether you allow tracking:

  • If you allow it, the request also carries the device advertising identifier (IDFA). We may use it for advertising. The identifier is not linked to your name, an account, or any other data in the game.
  • If you decline, the game never reads or sends the advertising identifier. The journal page keeps the device time, and the game works in full.

Like any network request, contacting our server reveals your device's IP address to it. Your photographs, voice notes, coordinates, entry text, and progress are not part of the request and never leave your device. Apart from that request, we receive and keep no data about you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • Field journal entries — the image of the floor you uncovered, the results of the dig, and the note you typed.
  • A photograph you took with the camera or picked from your library. A copy is placed in the journal; the original in your library is left untouched.
  • A voice note — the audio file of what you dictated.
  • The coordinates of a find spot, if you chose to tag the entry.
  • Progress — seals earned, best results per trench, and your open trench record.
  • Settings — sound, site ambience, haptics, the time of the daily reminder, and which hints you have already seen.

None of it is uploaded anywhere. Any attachment can be removed from an entry on its own, an entry can be deleted outright, and progress can be reset in the game's settings. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

Device permissions

Every permission is optional, is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and declining it leaves the rest of the game fully playable.

  • Camera — to photograph a find for a journal entry. The picture is kept in the journal on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Microphone — to dictate a voice note for an entry. The recording is kept in the journal on your device; no speech recognition is performed and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Photo library (read) — so you can attach a picture you already have. Only the images you open are read, and the one you choose is copied into the journal. You may grant access to a limited selection of photos.
  • Photo library (add) — to save the uncovered mosaic or a journal page to your library, when you press Export yourself.
  • Location (while using the app) — read once, when you tag a find spot, to roughly 100 metres. The coordinates are written into that journal entry and stay on your device. There is no background tracking, and the coordinates can be removed from the entry with one tap.
  • Notifications — a local reminder only, scheduled on your device at the time you pick. We do not use a push service and cannot send you messages.
  • Background audio — so the site ambience and voice note playback are not cut off when the app goes to the background. It can be switched off in the settings.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — asked once, before the first time check of a journal page. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Tracking and advertising

Tessera contains no advertising and no analytics. The device advertising identifier is sent to our server only with your permission, as described above, and we may use it for advertising. We share nothing with data brokers. Declining tracking, or withdrawing it in iOS Settings, does not affect the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services; it uses Apple's system frameworks only. The only server the game contacts is our own, api.platform-core.com: it receives the time check request and, with your permission, the advertising identifier. We do not pass that data on to third parties.

Your rights

Apart from the advertising identifier received with your consent, which is not linked to your identity, we hold no data about you. Everything created in the game is on your device and under your control. You can stop the advertising identifier from being sent in iOS Settings, and reset the identifier there too. If you would like to contact us about data protection, use the address below.
